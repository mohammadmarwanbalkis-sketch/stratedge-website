02The firm

Independent advice, delivered at senior level.

Stratedge was established to serve a specific need in the UAE market: businesses with real ambition, moving quickly, that require analytical depth without the overhead and distance of a large consulting house.

We keep engagements deliberately senior and deliberately focused. The people who scope the work are the people who deliver it, which shortens the path from question to answer and keeps accountability in one place.

  • Direct access to senior judgement throughout the engagement.
  • Independent of any product, platform or commission arrangement.
  • Scope, timeline and fees agreed in writing before work begins.
03Direction

Where we are going, and how we get there.

A vision without a method is an ambition. Both are stated here so clients can hold us to them.

V / 01

Our vision

To be the advisory firm that businesses in the region turn to when a decision genuinely matters — recognised for clarity, independence and the quality of our thinking.

M / 01

Our mission

To help business owners and leadership teams make better decisions, faster — combining rigorous analysis with practical commercial judgement, and staying involved until the plan is working.

04What we stand for

Six principles that govern our work.

These are not aspirations. They are the standards we hold ourselves to on every engagement, and the basis on which we expect to be judged.

Integrity

We tell clients what we believe to be true, including when it is not what they hoped to hear. Advice loses its value the moment it becomes agreeable.

Rigour

Every conclusion is traceable to evidence. Assumptions are written down, tested and open to challenge.

Discretion

Commercial information stays confidential. We treat client material as we would our own.

Practicality

A strategy that cannot be resourced is not a strategy. We plan against the capacity you actually have.

Partnership

We work alongside your team rather than around it, so capability stays in the business after we leave.

Accountability

We define what success looks like at the start, and measure ourselves against it at the end.

05How we think

Three commitments behind every engagement.

Consulting is a crowded market. These commitments are how we intend to be different, and they are written into how we scope and deliver.

C / 01

We start with the business, not the framework

Models are useful only after the facts are understood. We begin with your numbers, your market and your constraints.

C / 02

We show the reasoning

You receive the logic, the data and the assumptions — not only the conclusion. That is what makes a recommendation defensible.

C / 03

We stay until it is working

Handover includes the measures, the owners and the reporting required for your team to run the plan without us.

06Leadership

A single point of senior accountability.

Engagements are led personally, from the first briefing through to handover. To discuss a challenge in confidence, a direct conversation is the fastest route.
