What makes a financial model credible to an investor?

That its assumptions are visible, sourced and testable. Experienced reviewers do not audit your arithmetic; they interrogate what you assumed and how sensitive the outcome is to being wrong. A model whose drivers cannot be traced and flexed reads as advocacy rather than analysis.

How should pricing be set?

From the value delivered and the alternatives available to the buyer, constrained by cost. Most pricing in practice is set from cost alone, or inherited and never revisited, which is why a structured pricing review often finds margin without requiring a single additional sale.

What is investor readiness in practice?

Being able to answer the questions before they are asked: unit economics, the path to the numbers you are projecting, what breaks the model, and what the capital specifically buys. The documentation follows from that clarity rather than substituting for it.

What the work covers

Financial work translates a strategic plan into numbers that hold up under examination. The model is built around the drivers that actually move the business, with scenarios that show the range of outcomes rather than a single line that implies false precision.

Feasibility and investment case work assembles the commercial argument: what the opportunity is worth, what it costs to pursue, what returns are realistic under stated assumptions, and under what conditions it does not work. Pricing architecture examines how price is set, whether it reflects delivered value, and where margin is being given away. Unit economics analysis establishes whether growth improves or erodes profitability — a question a surprising number of growing businesses cannot answer.

Where the numbers do not support the plan, the engagement says so. Producing a model that flatters a decision already taken is not advisory work.

Sensitivity is where the value is

The base case is the least interesting part of a financial model. What matters is which assumptions the outcome is most sensitive to, because those are the ones that need managing, monitoring or de-risking before the capital is committed.

A well-built model tells you the two or three variables that decide whether this works. That output is often more useful to a management team than the projection itself, because it converts a static plan into a set of things to watch.
