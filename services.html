07Engagement models

Three ways to work with us.

Every engagement is quoted individually. Scope, timeline and fees are confirmed in writing before any work begins.

E / 01

Diagnostic

A focused review of a single question — a market, a margin problem, an operating model — delivered as findings and recommendations.

  • Defined scope and fixed fee
  • Structured findings report
  • Prioritised recommendations
  • Executive presentation
E / 02

Project engagement

End-to-end work on a defined objective: strategy development, market entry, feasibility or an operational redesign.

  • Full four-phase method
  • Analysis, options and business case
  • Implementation roadmap
  • Governance and measurement
E / 03

Retained advisory

Ongoing senior counsel for owners and leadership teams who want continuity of judgement as decisions arise.

  • Agreed monthly commitment
  • Standing leadership access
  • Performance review cadence
  • Priority scheduling

08Method

The same four phases, every time.

The discipline is identical whether the engagement runs for two weeks or six months. Only the depth changes.

01

Understand the business as it is

Performance data, market context and structured conversations with the people who run the business. The output is an honest diagnostic — including the findings that are uncomfortable.

Diagnostic report · Baseline metrics

02

Build the options and the case

Realistic strategic options, tested against capacity and capital, with a recommended direction and the reasoning shown. Every recommendation carries a business case.

Turn decisions into execution

Strategy fails at implementation. The plan converts into owners, milestones and measures, and we stay close to the leadership team while the first phase is executed.

Reporting that shows whether the strategy is working, performance reviewed against the baseline, and a handover that lets your team run the model without us.
